Kedarnath


Nestled in the Himalayas along the banks of the Mandakini River, Kedarnath is a sacred town in Uttarakhand, India. It is known for its spiritual atmosphere, breathtaking mountain views, and the ancient Kedarnath Temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Top 5 Attractions in Kedarnath

  1. Kedarnath Temple – The holy shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva.
  2. Vasuki Tal – A beautiful high-altitude lake surrounded by snow-clad peaks.
  3. Gaurikund – The starting point of the trek to Kedarnath and a sacred hot spring.
  4. Bhairavnath Temple – A temple dedicated to Bhairav, the guardian deity of Kedarnath.
  5. Chorabari Tal (Gandhi Sarovar) – A serene glacial lake near Kedarnath.

Must-Try Foods in Kedarnath

  • Rajma Chawal – A hearty meal perfect for cold mountain weather.
  • Aloo Paratha – Stuffed flatbread served with butter and curd.
  • Kadhi Pakora – A delicious and tangy local favorite.
  • Maggi – A comforting bowl of noodles enjoyed by trekkers in the hills.
  • Gulab Jamun – A sweet treat to end your meal on a warm note.
