Holiday Destination


My holiday destination is Kedarnath. Located near the Mandakini River, Kedarnath offers a peaceful and spiritual atmosphere that attracts pilgrims and nature lovers alike.

Top 5 Attractions in Kedarnath

  1. Kedarnath Temple
  2. Vasuki Tal
  3. Gaurikund
  4. Shankaracharya Samadhi
  5. Bhairav Temple

Must-Try Foods in Kedarnath

  • Rajma Chawal
  • Dal Bati
  • Puran Poli
  • Idli
  • Aloo Paratha
